## The short version

I build tools that make life harder for scammers.

I'm a self-taught developer in **Ghana**. I started by watching people around me lose money to phishing links, fake mobile-money messages and cloned login pages, and I got tired of being angry about it. So I built the thing I wanted to exist: **ScamWatch Ghana**, a platform where anyone can check a message, a link or a number before they trust it.

**Security research** under a `@wearehackerone.com` alias, in scope, by the rules. **Agent engineering:** writing specs instead of vibes - doctrine, verification gates and evidence standards that keep an AI agent honest when nobody is watching. **Teaching:** opening the door for the next two people behind me, because one investigator in Ghana closes one scam and ten investigators close a network.

## How I verify before I submit

<details>
<summary><b>The standard I hold myself and my agents to</b> (click)</summary>

<br>

- **Evidence, not adjectives.** A finding is real when it reproduces from a clean session.
- **Preflight first.** In scope, no third-party data, no DoS, own test accounts only.
- **Minimum necessary proof.** Two accounts, one request, the smallest thing that demonstrates impact.
- **No secrets in the report.** Redact tokens, hashes and personal data.
- **One report, one root cause.** If a finding is a chain, it is one report scored at the top of the chain.
- **Honest coverage gaps.** Every report says what it could not see.

</details>
